Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SETHU ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you interact with our website and waitlist.

1. Information We Collect

During the current pre-launch phase, we collect the following information through our waitlist form:

  • Full Name — to identify you
  • Email Address — to communicate updates about SETHU
  • Timestamp — when you joined the waitlist
  • Source — how you reached the waitlist (e.g. website)

We do not currently collect financial data, business information, professional profiles or assessment responses. As the SETHU platform develops, this policy will be updated to reflect any additional data collection.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Manage the SETHU early access waitlist
  • Send you updates about SETHU's development and launch
  • Communicate early access opportunities

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Data Retention

We retain waitlist data for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described above, or until you request deletion.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for communications at any time

6. External Profile Data

SETHU does not scrape or process external professional profile information (such as LinkedIn data) without appropriate user consent and compliance with applicable platform rules. Any future integration with external profiles will require explicit opt-in consent.

7. Cookies

The current SETHU website does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. This may change as the platform develops, and this policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as SETHU develops. Material changes will be communicated to waitlist members via email. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please contact us through the channels that will be provided when the platform launches.
