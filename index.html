Important Principle

The highest score is not necessarily the best match.

A person with a Personal Score of 95 may be excellent generally but a poor fit for a particular business. A person with a Personal Score of 82 may have a much stronger Match Score for that business.

Personal Score ≠ Match Score. Business Score ≠ Match Score.
The Match Engine is a separate layer.
